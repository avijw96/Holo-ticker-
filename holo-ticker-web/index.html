Settings: server and grading fees

Your Holo Ticker server holds the Claude and PriceCharting keys (see the README). The address and app key are saved in this browser only.

Per-card fees in USD. Defaults are each company's cheapest standard tier as reported in mid-2026 (PSA's Value tiers were paused). Change them to what you'd actually pay. Shipping is round-trip per card, including insurance.

Prices come from PriceCharting's sold-listing averages and refresh every 30 minutes while this page is open; most card markets only move a few times a day. Your collection is saved in this browser only. Grades 7–9 are mostly PSA sales. Grade estimates from photos can't see fine surface scratches or print lines, so treat them as a first screen, not a promise. Not financial advice.
